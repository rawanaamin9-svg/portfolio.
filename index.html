MEET THE ARTIST

Hi, I'm Rawan.

I'm an Egyptian visual artist and animator, and a graduate of the Faculty of Fine Arts, specializing in Graphic Design and Animation. I work across character design, illustration, and visual storytelling. I see art as a way to explore emotions, communicate ideas, and present the world from different perspectives. Through illustration and animation, I aim to transform concepts into expressive characters and engaging visual worlds that tell meaningful stories and leave a lasting impression.
